FAQ

Straight answers before you fill anything out

Are you a vending company?

No. We're a matching and referral service. We don't own, place, stock, or service any equipment. We connect you with a vetted vending or convenience services provider, and that provider works with you directly.

Is there a cost to my business?

Typically, no. Providers in our network pay for qualified introductions, so getting matched costs your business nothing and you're under no obligation to move forward.

What happens after I submit the form?

A real person from our team reaches out within one business day, asks a few questions about your space, and introduces you to a matched provider. Nothing is signed with us.

Who signs the agreement and handles service?

The provider you're matched with. You sign directly with them, and they handle equipment, stocking, and service. We never sign the hosting agreement and we're not part of day-to-day operations.

Do you work with schools?

Yes. Schools are a big part of who we help. Mention that you're a school on the form and we'll look for a provider that works with schools in your area.

Which areas do you cover?

All six New England states: Maine, New Hampshire, Vermont, Massachusetts, Rhode Island, and Connecticut.
